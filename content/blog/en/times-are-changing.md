## Hey everyone

The last time I actually wrote a blog post was on the old WordPress site. So please cut me some slack.

After the recent escalations with PHP2Shell, the reaction from the fucking WELT, and the (very likely) increase in such “apocalyptic” events in the coming years, I just wanted to vent a little.

I've had the chance to evaluate a few different companies' approaches to software development. Pretty much all of those that focus on “custom” software development are on the verge of becoming obsolete in light of current developments in AI agents. And by that I don't mean that the quality of the agents is all that great… But CEOs, COOs, and CTOs don't give a damn about developers' technical assessments and rely on AI agents for advice.

What does this mean for us? Well, I, for one, am still looking at developer jobs because programming and designing architectures just fulfill me. In the long run, though, cybersecurity and hardware integration jobs are probably the only options left to us.

Brave New World. I'll start by making my subnets less dependent on the internet.

Hang in there.

Yours, Josun
